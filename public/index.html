In Detail

Our interior detailing services — in detail

Every sub-service is performed by trained professionals using premium products and commercial-grade equipment.

Steam Cleaning

Professional steam cleaning for your car's interior

Our high-temperature steam process penetrates deep into every surface — dashboards, door panels, center consoles, cup holders and crevices. Steam sanitizes and lifts embedded grime without harsh chemicals, making it safe for all interior materials including leather, vinyl, plastic and fabric.

Key Benefits

  • Kills 99.9% of bacteria and allergens
  • Chemical-free sanitization
  • Reaches tight crevices and vents
  • Safe for all interior surfaces
  • Removes sticky residue and buildup

Perfect For

Allergy sufferers, families with children, rideshare drivers, and anyone who wants a deep-sanitized cabin without chemical residue.

Professional Vacuum & Air Blowout

Thorough vacuuming & high-pressure air blowout

We use commercial-grade vacuums and high-pressure air tools to remove every trace of dirt, dust, crumbs and debris from your vehicle. Our process covers seats, carpets, floor mats, trunk, under seats and between-seat crevices — areas most car washes miss entirely.

Key Benefits

  • Commercial-grade suction power
  • High-pressure air for vents and crevices
  • Under-seat and between-seat cleaning
  • Trunk and cargo area included
  • Floor mats deep cleaned separately

Perfect For

Daily commuters, pet owners dealing with hair and dander, parents with crumb-filled back seats, and anyone overdue for a thorough interior clean.

Shampoo & Hot-Water Extraction

Deep carpet & upholstery shampoo

Hot-water extraction injects a cleaning solution deep into carpets, floor mats and fabric seats, then extracts it along with embedded dirt, stains and odors. Unlike surface cleaning, this is what truly lifts set-in stains — and it's required for full stain removal.

Key Benefits

  • Lifts deep, set-in stains and spills
  • Removes odors at the source
  • Restores fabric color and texture
  • True extraction, not surface-only
  • Carpets, floor mats & fabric seats

Perfect For

Coffee and drink spills, kid and pet messes, gym sweat, and anyone with stained or tired-looking upholstery.

Leather & Upholstery Conditioning

Clean, condition & protect

We gently clean leather and upholstery, then condition it to restore natural suppleness and add a layer of UV protection. This prevents the drying, cracking and fading that come from sun exposure and daily use — preserving both look and resale value.

Key Benefits

  • Deep-cleans leather and trim
  • Restores natural suppleness
  • UV protection against fading
  • Prevents cracking and drying
  • Subtle, non-greasy protective finish

Perfect For

Luxury and leather-equipped vehicles, sun-exposed daily drivers, and owners who want to preserve resale value.

Interior Surface Detailing

Dashboards, panels, trim & glass

Every hard surface is detailed by hand — dashboards, door panels, consoles, vents, buttons and interior glass. We finish with a natural, non-greasy look (no sticky shine) and streak-free glass for a cabin that feels showroom-fresh.

Key Benefits

  • Dust and grime removed from every surface
  • Vents, buttons and crevices detailed
  • Streak-free interior glass
  • Natural, non-greasy finish
  • Trim and plastics refreshed

Perfect For

Anyone who wants a showroom-fresh cabin, dusty daily drivers, and detail-oriented owners.
